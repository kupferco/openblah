OpenBlah GitHub

Open-source voice layer

Talk together. An AI joins the room.

A voice call where the AI listens quietly, answers when you ask, runs tasks, and takes notes. In 30 seconds:

  1. 1

    Start a call

    Tap once and you are talking. No account for guests.

  2. 2

    Invite people

    Share a link, even mid-conversation. Nobody restarts the call.

  3. 3

    The agent is in the room

    It listens, answers when asked, and takes notes. It knows when to stay quiet.

How it works

A voice SDK for apps

Packages for the web and for React Native, so you can add group voice with an AI agent to your own product.

Open source, MIT

Small, no lock-in. Choose your own model: Gemini, OpenAI or Claude.

Built on LiveKit

Real-time WebRTC audio, with a speech pipeline tuned for natural turn-taking.
